# NEF–G-3–GerChain — Management Review Requirements

**Status:** Governance framework — review not performed

## 1. Objective

Define the evidence and decisions required for management to periodically review the suitability, adequacy and effectiveness of the ISMS.

## 2. Required inputs

Management review should consider, as applicable:

- previous review actions;
- changes in internal and external context;
- interested-party and legal/regulatory requirements;
- information-security objectives and performance;
- risk assessment and treatment status;
- audit results;
- incidents and corrective actions;
- supplier/security performance;
- resource adequacy;
- opportunities for continual improvement.

## 3. Required decisions

The review record shall document decisions concerning:

- changes to the ISMS;
- risk treatment and residual-risk acceptance;
- resources;
- priorities;
- corrective actions;
- improvement opportunities;
- readiness for independent assessment.

## 4. Evidence

The controlled record should identify date, participants/roles, inputs reviewed, decisions, action owners, deadlines and closure evidence.

## 5. Gate

**G8 Management Review: NOT PERFORMED.** Formal management review evidence is required before final certification-readiness closure.
