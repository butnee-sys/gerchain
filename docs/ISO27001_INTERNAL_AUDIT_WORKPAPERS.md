# NEF–G-3–GerChain — Internal Audit Workpapers

**Status:** Audit framework — execution not started

## 1. Objective

Provide a repeatable internal-audit method for evaluating whether the defined ISMS requirements are implemented, operating and supported by objective evidence.

## 2. Independence

The auditor shall be sufficiently independent of the activity being audited. A person should not provide the final assurance over controls for which that person is the sole implementer or owner.

## 3. Audit scope

The audit shall cover the approved ISMS scope, applicable information assets, risk treatment, Statement of Applicability, selected Annex A controls, organizational controls and required evidence.

## 4. Audit procedure

`Plan → Review criteria → Sample evidence → Interview responsible persons → Test operation → Record finding → Classify → Correct → Verify closure → Report`

## 5. Sampling

Sampling shall be risk-based and traceable. The workpaper should identify:

- control/domain;
- criterion;
- sample selected;
- evidence reference;
- test performed;
- result;
- finding severity;
- responsible owner;
- corrective action;
- closure evidence.

## 6. Finding classification

- **Conformity:** objective evidence supports implementation and operation.
- **Observation:** weakness or improvement opportunity without sufficient basis for nonconformity.
- **Minor nonconformity:** limited failure against an applicable requirement.
- **Major nonconformity:** systemic or significant failure affecting the ISMS or required control outcome.

## 7. CORE boundary

GerChain CORE technical evidence may be sampled as supporting evidence. A technical test PASS shall not by itself establish an organizational ISMS control as conformant.

## 8. Outputs

The internal audit package shall include an approved plan, completed workpapers, findings register, corrective-action register, closure verification and final internal-audit report.

## 9. Gate

**G7 Internal Audit: NOT STARTED.** Execution requires an approved ISMS scope, named auditor, applicable criteria and evidence set.
