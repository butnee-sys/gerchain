# NEF–G-3–GerChain — Legal and Regulatory Requirements

**Status:** Controlled requirements — legal register pending

## 1. Purpose

Establish a traceable process for identifying legal, regulatory and contractual information-security obligations applicable to the ISMS scope.

## 2. Requirements register

For each applicable requirement record:

- source/law/regulation/contract;
- subject matter;
- applicable process/system;
- responsible owner;
- required control;
- evidence required;
- review frequency;
- change-monitoring method.

## 3. Areas for assessment

The legal review should consider, as applicable:

- personal/data protection;
- cybersecurity and information-security obligations;
- electronic records and signatures;
- financial/escrow/payment-related regulation;
- intellectual property;
- contractual confidentiality;
- tax/accounting implications;
- cross-border data/value-flow requirements;
- sector-specific obligations.

This document is a control framework and is not legal advice or a determination that any particular regulation applies.

## 4. Evidence

The evidence package should contain an approved legal/regulatory register, responsible owner, review record, changes identified, and resulting control decisions.

## 5. Gate

**Legal/regulatory compliance: MISSING — formal applicability review and maintained requirements register required.**
