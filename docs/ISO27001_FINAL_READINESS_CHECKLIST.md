# NEF–G-3–GerChain — Final ISO/IEC 27001 Readiness Checklist

**Status:** Readiness checklist — not a certification statement

## A. Governance

- [ ] ISMS scope formally approved
- [ ] information-security policy approved
- [ ] roles and responsibilities assigned
- [ ] interested parties and requirements documented
- [ ] risk criteria approved

## B. Risk and applicability

- [ ] complete information-asset register
- [ ] complete risk assessment
- [ ] risk treatment plan approved
- [ ] residual risks accepted by authorized management
- [ ] Statement of Applicability approved

## C. Control operation

- [ ] IAM/MFA evidence complete
- [ ] privileged-access evidence complete
- [ ] cryptographic-key management evidence complete
- [ ] backup/restore test completed
- [ ] incident-response exercise completed
- [ ] supplier-security evidence complete
- [ ] physical-security evidence complete
- [ ] legal/regulatory register complete
- [ ] personnel-security evidence complete

## D. Performance and assurance

- [ ] security objectives and measurements established
- [ ] evidence register complete
- [ ] internal audit completed
- [ ] findings recorded
- [ ] corrective actions completed or formally accepted
- [ ] effectiveness verified
- [ ] management review completed
- [ ] independent re-performance completed

## E. Final assessment

- [ ] independent/certification assessment scope agreed
- [ ] assessment evidence room prepared
- [ ] findings closed or formally managed
- [ ] final certification decision received, if certification is pursued

## F. Non-negotiable statements

Until the above requirements are objectively satisfied, external materials shall use wording such as:

> “MNS ISO/IEC 27001:2023-д нийцүүлэх ISMS хэрэгжилтийн бэлтгэл болон техникийн хамгаалалтын суурь хяналтууд хэрэгжиж байна.”

They shall not state “ISO 27001 certified” or “fully conformant.”

## G. Protection of CORE

The CORE technical baseline remains controlled. ISO readiness work must not weaken branch protection, auditability, transaction integrity, cryptographic controls or source-code confidentiality.
